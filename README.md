# node map

### → **[Open the map](https://guccibum.github.io/nodemap/)**

A node-based map of media — images, video, audio and notes wired together on an
infinite canvas. Pan, zoom, drag nodes, follow the connections.

**https://guccibum.github.io/nodemap/**

---

Press **F** to fit the whole map on screen. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to
move around. Nodes show a preview until you zoom in close enough to load the
real file.

Anything you move or change is saved to your own browser only.

---

This repo holds the site itself. The media is attached to the
[media release](https://github.com/guccibum/nodemap/releases/tag/media).
