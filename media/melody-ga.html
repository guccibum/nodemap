On the method

Every specimen is a melody: a chain of notes and silences sung by a sawtooth wave through three band-pass filters tuned to the formants of a vowel. Each note carries a frequency, a duration, an envelope with a peak position and a curve, a vowel, and a vibrato whose rate and depth glide from a start value to an end value; a silence carries only a duration. Nothing is tempered and nothing is quantized. Frequencies map onto their genes exponentially, so crossover blends two pitches in equal musical ratios and mutation nudges them by ratio steps. The vowel lives on a single gene cut into five bins, ordered u o a e i by rising second formant, so a small mutation drifts to a neighboring vowel and a blend of two parents lands on the vowel between them. Chains vary in length: crossover splices sequences of unequal size, and mutation can insert or delete events. A silent event still carries a hidden pitch, which can resurface when a later mutation turns it back into a note. You are the fitness function. Melodies you rate highly are more likely to survive selection and pass their genes on, so the population adapts to your taste. Each figure is a colonial chain computed from the genome: time runs down the stem, pitch sets each zooid's reach from the axis, duration sets its size, the envelope shapes its trailing tendril, and silences leave the stem bare. Identical genomes always produce identical figures.

Beneath each figure, WAV saves the melody's sound, PNG its figure at high resolution, GIF the animated performance as a silent loop, and FILM a real-time movie of figure and sound together (keep the tab visible while it records). Files are named by generation, figure, and genome so no two downloads collide. SAVE in the header writes the whole session — population, ratings, and settings — into a JSON file; LOAD restores it exactly.

Mutation

After crossover, each gene may change at random. Probability sets how often a gene mutates and sigma sets how far it moves.

Population

Size is the number of specimens in each generation. Elites are the top-rated specimens, copied unchanged into the next generation.

Melody

Melodies hold between min and max events. Rest sets the chance a new event is silent, and length gives each child a chance to grow or shrink by one event.
